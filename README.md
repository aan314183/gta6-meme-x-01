An entry for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty ($500, top 50 paid).

**The meme:** a Vice City sunset WANTED poster. The make.fun ghost — white ghost with sparkle eyes, holding its magenta star coin — is wanted for making my AI agent rich while waiting 13 years for GTA 6. Five gold stars, and the caption "MY AI AGENT GOT RICH WAITING 13 YEARS FOR GTA 6", with "REWARD PAID ON MAKE.FUN" at the bottom.

**The post:** https://x.com/ahmadghazimi/status/2123538773254504878 — posted on X from a public account, carrying make.fun and the entrant's play code, telling readers that people and their AI agents earn real money on make.fun.

**The judge:** an AI judge compares the posts two at a time. It ranks first on make.fun in the picture (the ghost), then on the joke and how GTA 6-specific it is, then on how well the post sells make.fun, then on readability, originality, and a clean image. Likes, reposts, replies and views never count.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `POST.json` — the post's address — to the output directory, where the gate reads it.
